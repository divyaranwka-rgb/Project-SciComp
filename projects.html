School project

Lung Cancer Risk Detection Model

A school project applying machine learning to healthcare: a logistic regression model trained on a 310-patient dataset of lifestyle and clinical risk factors (smoking, anxiety, chronic disease, wheezing, chest pain, and more) to classify lung cancer risk. Built the full pipeline: data cleaning and encoding, a 70/30 train-test split, model training, and evaluation with a confusion matrix, accuracy, precision, and recall.

Built with Python, pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn.
